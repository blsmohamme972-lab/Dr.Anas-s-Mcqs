# Report — Cardiothoracic Surgery Board Review 2015

For AI agents and editors who maintain this book. Not shown in the app.

- Data file: `data/questions/cardiothoracic-2015.json`
- Questions in the app: 2000

## Book-level notes

- Not yet medically reviewed: no explanations; answer keys are as in the source; topics were assigned by keywords and then a manual pass over every question stem, and should be confirmed during medical review.
- This is a mixed cardiothoracic book: questions are filed under cardiac, thoracic or (a few) vascular topics according to what each stem tests; the book's own specialty is set to cardiac only for the cover colour.
- The source is divided into 10 untitled sections of 200 questions; they are kept as chapters ('Section 1 (Q1–200)' …) so the book can be studied in parts.
- Options are numbered 1–4 in the source; in the app they are lettered A–D in the same order (1 = A … 4 = D), and the key was converted the same way. Every key is confirmed in the source by the repeated answer text.
- All 2000 questions have an answer key; none were removed.
- Many questions are general surgery, critical-care, pharmacology or ethics items with no exact match among the 66 topics; each was filed under the closest topic (see the per-question notes).
